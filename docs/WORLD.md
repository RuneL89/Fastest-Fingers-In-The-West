# WORLD — Tombwood Creek

## The Town

**Tombwood Creek** — named in the spirit of Tombstone, Deadwood and Cripple Creek. A classic Wild West town: both a **gold mining town and a cattle town**, sitting in the middle of nowhere with the classic wooden buildings along a dusty main street.

Era-flavor: the classic 1880s frontier. Gold dug from the nearby mine, cattle driven through to market, one main street of false-fronted wooden buildings, a church steeple and a water tower on the skyline.

## Scope of the World

The world exists as the **overhead town map** plus the **duel backgrounds**. No outside world, no travel, no larger geography needed. The player picks a tormented location on the map and is straight into the duel there, with that location's background and its suffering NPCs visible. Larger world-building is deliberately out of scope.

## Constant Conditions

- **Time: high noon** at every duel. Same harsh summer sun, same long shadows.
- **Weather: everlasting dusty summer.** No rain, no snow, no dusk.
- **Gang size: exactly six.** Five goons and Black Bart. No other gang members — backgrounds show only townsfolk, never anonymous gangsters.

## The Gang

The Buffalo Gang: five male goons with no hierarchy (biker-club vibe) and Black Bart as leader — larger, meaner, tougher, full buffalo head for a hat. They rolled into a peaceful town of good-natured folk and took over the places above purely to drink, gamble, and lord it over people. Wrong place, wrong time. See CHARACTERS.md for full identities.

## The Mood

Earnest, not grim. The town is inconvenienced, bullied and robbed, not brutalized. When an area is freed, life snaps cheerfully back to normal. Tone reference: Punch-Out!!'s fond, larger-than-life characterizations.
