# Story

Fastest Fingers in the West — Story Mode design document.

_Status: decisions locked for v1 (2026-11-21). Deferred items marked OPEN._

## Premise

A quiet little frontier town of simple, good-natured folks. The Buffalo Gang
rolled in one day, liked what they found, and never left. They drink, fight,
harass the businesses, and make life miserable, and the townsfolk, being
peaceful people, never found a way to resist. So the town does what such
towns do: they send for a new sheriff. That is the player. The story opens
with the gang already entrenched and the sheriff arriving.

## Tone

Earnest. Real menace, real relief, no wink at the camera. The one slice of
Wild-West myth allowed is the sheriff fantasy itself.

## The player character

There is no sheriff character. The player *is* the sheriff. NPCs and enemies
address the player directly, facing the screen. No backstory, no face, no
voice of his own; his dialogue is typed words and drawn guns.

No old-sheriff subplot of any kind.

## The gang

The Buffalo Gang: five goons and a leader. No ranks, no lieutenants, no
structure. Think a biker club that rolled into town: each goon has staked out
a spot (saloon, bank, stable, store, church, or whatever the five locations
become) and makes that place miserable in his own way.

The name "Buffalo" has no in-world origin. The one piece of gang iconography:
the leader's hat is a buffalo head. That carries all the villain identity.

The leader, Black Bart, is simply a larger, meaner, tougher opponent. No
tragic past, no scheme, no charisma narrative. He is the biggest goon, and
the town flinches hardest at his name.

## Structure (v1 scope)

Five areas, five goons, one leader. Each goon holds a location and makes it
his. Beating a goon frees that spot, and the citizens there come out with a
short, unique thank-you. The arc is a simple "free the town" ladder: five
duels of equal difficulty, then Black Bart with five lives instead of three.

Happy ending: the town cheers, Bart's buffalo hat hits the dirt.

## Story text budget

- Prologue: 4 to 5 sentences, no more.
- Between duels: one short thankful line per freed area, each citizen a
  distinct character.
- That is the entire text payload of story mode.
